How to play

  • Steer Drag your finger on the field, move your mouse, or use the arrow keys.
  • Spin move Tap Spin, double-tap the field, or press the space bar. Nobody can pull your flags mid-spin.
  • Score Touchdown = 7 points. Donuts = 1 bonus point. Close calls build combos and XP.
  • Flags You have 3. Lose them all and the game is over.
