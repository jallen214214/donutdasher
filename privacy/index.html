Privacy

The short version: no accounts, no names.

Donut Dasher makes free games for kids. Here’s exactly what happens today when you play, and what we’ll do before anything changes.

We don’t ask who you are

Kids don’t type a name, email, age or anything else about themselves to play. There’s no account to make.

Progress stays on your device

Best scores, unlocked looks, badges and settings are saved in your browser’s storage on your own device. They aren’t sent to us. To erase them, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

The Flag Dash leaderboard

Adding a score to the monthly leaderboard is optional. If you do, we save:

  • a nickname the game made up from a list of fun words, like “Turbo Taco 42”
  • your score, how many touchdowns you scored, and when
  • a random code the game created on your device, so two players with the same nickname stay separate

Only the nickname and score are shown to others. Past months are kept so we can show each month’s champion.

To stop spam, our server keeps a scrambled version of your device’s internet address for about an hour. It can’t be turned back into the address.

Ads and analytics

Right now the site shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking cookies. If we add either in the future, we’ll update this page before it happens and follow the laws that protect children online, including the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We don’t sell personal information.

The fine print

Our pages use fonts from Google Fonts. To send the fonts, Google’s servers see your device’s internet address. Like every website, our web host keeps standard server logs, such as internet addresses and browser type, for security.

Perihelion’s “Share” button only opens your own device’s share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share.
